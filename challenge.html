4 problems that look alike. Only one is yours.

When email disappoints, the symptom is almost always the same: "my list isn't working." The real cause changes completely.

Build
You're attracting the wrong people
Too few relevant subscribers, or weak acquisition. The problem isn't list size — it's recruitment.
Warm
You stopped writing, they stopped opening
Subscribers exist, but communication became inconsistent. The relationship went cold, not dead.
Revive
Part of your list has gone dark
A meaningful chunk of the list is inactive. Not dead — just not in the conversation lately.
Convert
They read you, but don't buy
The audience exists, sometimes even engages — but customer activity stays weak. The path to purchase isn't clear.
The 1,000-more test

If you added 1,000 people identical to the ones already on your list, would that solve your problem? If the answer is no, it isn't size — it's the system. This challenge starts with Warm and Revive — the two paths for a list that's gone quiet.
